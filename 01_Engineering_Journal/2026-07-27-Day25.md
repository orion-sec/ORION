# Engineering Decisions

Decided not to immediately redesign the Threat Engine.

Instead:

Complete Threat Intelligence enrichment first.

Once stable:

Improve Threat Reasoning.

Finally:

Improve Contextual Risk calculations.

This preserves ORION's layered architecture and minimizes unnecessary refactoring.

---

# Lessons Learned

Engineering utilities save significant manual effort.

Good architecture allows substantial functionality to be added without modifying unrelated modules.

Separating:

Collection

↓

Reasoning

↓

Decision Making

creates a cleaner and more maintainable investigation pipeline.

Today's work demonstrated the value of building modular systems before integrating external services.
