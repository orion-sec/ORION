## 1. Day 38 Objective

The primary objective for Day 38 was to move ORION closer to the operational behaviour of a real SOC analyst by ensuring that security alerts/incidents entering from Microsoft Sentinel are investigated independently.

The key architectural principle established today is:

> Every incoming security alert or incident receives its own investigation context. Correlation is enrichment, not automatic case merging.

This is important for ORION's intended production model where alerts continuously arrive in a SOC queue and must be individually triaged, investigated, dispositioned, closed, or escalated.
